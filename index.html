Village

MinecraftJava

Village follows a very similar flow to Among Us. Players will move around, do tasks, etc. until either a body is found or a meeting is called. Once a meeting is called, players will be teleported to the meeting teleport to discuss (I recommend using Discord for voice, but chat works fine). They are able to evict a player by majority vote, or just go onto the next day.

GitHub ↗ Spigot ↗

CxTokens

MinecraftJava

CxTokens adds a complete token-based economy to Spigot/Paper servers. Players can buy and sell items in a configurable store, bid against each other in a live auction house, place bounties on rivals, store money in physical vaults, share balances through PIN-protected token accounts, and take their chances in timed lotteries. Nearly every feature can be tweaked or disabled through config files.

GitHub ↗ Spigot ↗
